Directory entry. Title and current location confirmed on irs.gov on 21 August 2026; the linked page is authoritative for the current revision and instructions. The two conditions are conjunctive, in contrast to the full Form 990 thresholds, which are disjunctive — an asset-rich organisation with modest receipts files Form 990.
