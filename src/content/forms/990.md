Directory entry. Title and current location confirmed on irs.gov on 21 August 2026; the linked page is authoritative for the current revision and instructions. The return is due on the 15th day of the 5th month after the close of the tax year and must be filed electronically under IRC § 6033(n). Note the conjunction: either threshold on its own requires the full return.
