Directory entry. Title and current location confirmed on irs.gov on 18 August 2026. The IRS describes it as the application for an extension of time under IRC § 6161 for the tax shown or required to be shown on a return, or for an amount determined as a deficiency; the conditions under § 6161 are printed on the reverse of the application itself.
