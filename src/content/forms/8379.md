Directory entry. Title and current location confirmed on irs.gov on 18 August 2026; the linked page is authoritative for the current revision and instructions. The IRS describes it as the form the injured spouse on a jointly filed return uses to get back their share of the joint refund when the joint overpayment is applied to a past-due obligation of the other spouse.
