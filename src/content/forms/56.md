Directory entry. Title and current location confirmed on irs.gov on 18 August 2026. The IRS describes it as providing notification of the creation or termination of a fiduciary relationship under IRC § 6903, and giving notice of qualification under § 6036. A Form 2848 signed by the taxpayer does not survive their death; the fiduciary established by this form signs a fresh one.
