Directory entry. Confirmed on irs.gov on 18 August 2026 against *Understanding your CP2000 series notice*, which covers CP2000, CP2000A, CP2000B, CP2000C, CP2000D and CP2000E. The IRS states expressly that the notice is not a bill; a response is required by the date the notice gives, and disagreement is registered on the response form with supporting documentation.
